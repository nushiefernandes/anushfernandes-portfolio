An AI assistant that teaches you how to pick ripe fruits and vegetables at the market. Built for reducing food waste and building confidence at the market.

## How It Works

### Visual Analysis
Upload images asking "is this ripe?" and get detailed feedback on:
- Current ripeness stage
- Expected days until peak
- Signs to look for

### Knowledge Base
Ask "what to look for when buying mangoes" and get:
- Visual cues (color, skin texture)
- Tactile cues (firmness, weight)
- Smell indicators
- Seasonal considerations

### Adaptive Learning
The skill learns from your preferences over time:
- You like firmer avocados? It adjusts recommendations
- You prefer sweeter melons? It factors that in

## Example Queries

```
"Is this avocado ripe?"
[uploads photo]

"What should I look for when buying tomatoes?"

"How do I know if a watermelon is sweet?"

"This mango smells sweet but feels hard—should I buy it?"
```

## Why This Matters

Food waste often starts at the store:
- Buying produce that's already past peak
- Avoiding good produce because you're unsure
- Not knowing how to time ripening at home

This skill helps you buy smarter and waste less.
